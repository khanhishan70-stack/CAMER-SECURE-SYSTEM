Voice commands

Say "camera on" to turn Security Mode ON, "camera off" to turn it OFF, "voice on/off" to toggle Voice Control, and "Esta lock PC" to lock the PC instantly (name + lock, like Win+L - a plain "lock screen" alone won't lock). Speech is recognized locally on this PC (offline). While Voice Control is OFF the mic still listens for the wake word "voice on" only, so you never need to click to re-enable it. Use MUTE to fully shut the microphone down - then the AI hears absolutely no one until you click UNMUTE. The mute state is saved and stays effective even after the app restarts.
